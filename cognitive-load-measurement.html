Cognitive Load Measurement

Cognitive load measurement helps teams detect unnecessary complexity in text before it reaches users, customers, patients, or internal operators.

Open live cognitive load measurement demo Back to B1C3 landing

What Is Measured

The goal is practical readability diagnostics, not abstract scoring theater.

Complexity Signals

Sentence structure, density, and wording patterns that increase reading burden.

Clarity Risks

Segments likely to create confusion, misinterpretation, or drop-off.

Accessibility Impact

How language choices affect comprehension across audiences with different cognitive constraints.

Why Cognitive Load Measurement Matters

Teams usually evaluate writing by style preference. Cognitive load measurement adds a more inspectable layer, helping writers, reviewers, and decision makers focus on comprehension outcomes.

Public Sector and Compliance

Lower the risk of misunderstood instructions in policy, legal, and regulated communication.

Product and Support

Improve help center content, ticket responses, and in-app guidance to reduce rework and escalations.

Research and Education

Compare drafts and track whether communication gets easier to process over time.

Proof Surfaces

  1. Run the text through the live tool and inspect metric outputs.
  2. Revise wording based on high-friction segments.
  3. Re-measure and compare before/after clarity.

This turns cognitive load measurement into an operational workflow, not just a static score.
