B1C3 Philosophy

B1C3 is a diagnostic and intervention framework for mission drift in complex systems. It exists to restore alignment between goals, reality, ownership, coordination, and action.

Goals over mechanism

Process must serve outcomes, not replace them.

Reality over story

Claims matter only if they can survive contact with actual constraints.

Ownership over diffusion

Clear responsibility is how coordination becomes possible.

Primary focus

The most plausible near-term wedge is evidence-to-decision work for Baltic Sea restoration and adjacent public-interest systems, especially where there is fragmented evidence, weak traceability, and no clear single view of what to do next.

Best fit

Public authorities, Interreg partners, NGOs, and evidence translators who need traceability and priority structure.

Least fit

Core field science, sensor deployment, and generic outreach work without a decision problem attached.

Current story

B1C3 enters the mess, models what matters, and returns a clearer choice architecture.

What to sell

Movement and Collaboration

  1. Bring a messy system, a traceability gap, or a decision bottleneck.
  2. Contribute through a scoped diagnostic, not a vague partnership pitch.
  3. Carry the result into a clearer next decision and a reusable proof artifact.

B1C3 is open to collaborators and organizations who need complexity reduced into something they can defend, decide on, and act from.

Contact comes later. First the focus, then the proof, then the outreach.
